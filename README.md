# Vesper

Builds of Vesper, a player for your own IPTV account. Vesper includes no channels or content. There is no code here, only the finished apps.

## Download

- **Windows PC:** [Vesper-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Anarr9/vesper-releases/releases/latest/download/Vesper-Setup.exe). Windows may say it protected your PC the first time. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV:** [Vesper.apk](https://github.com/Anarr9/vesper-releases/releases/latest/download/Vesper.apk). With the Downloader app, enter `https://github.com/Anarr9/vesper-releases/releases/latest/download/Vesper.apk`.

Both links always give the newest version.

## Updates

Once Vesper is installed, it tells you when a new version is out. On a PC or an Android TV box, press **Update now** and your sources, favorites and settings stay as they are. Samsung TVs are updated from a computer.
